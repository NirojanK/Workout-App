Training Split Guide

PPL (Push Pull Legs)

Best for consistency and balanced growth. Train 5–6 days per week.

  • Push: Chest, Shoulders, Triceps
  • Pull: Back, Biceps
  • Legs: Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes

Bro Split

Focus on one muscle group per day. Great for isolating muscles.

  • Day 1: Chest
  • Day 2: Back
  • Day 3: Legs
  • Day 4: Arms

Upper / Lower Split

Great for beginners and strength focus.

  • Upper Body: Chest, Back, Arms
  • Lower Body: Legs, Glutes

Back Day

  • Deadlifts(3 sets of 5-8 reps)
  • Pullups(the king of back workouts, 3-4 sets until failure)
  • Seated Cable Rows( 3 to 4 sets of 8-12 reps)
  • Barbell Rows (2-4 sets of 5-10 reps)
  • Lat Pulldowns (3 sets of 7-10 reps)
  • Reverse cable flys (3 sets of 8-10 reps)

Chest Day

  • Bench Press (3 sets of 8-10 reps)
  • Push-ups (1 or 2 sets until failure)
  • Incline Dumbbell Press (3 sets of 8-10 reps)
  • Chest Flys (3 sets of 10-15 reps)
  • Dips (3 sets of 4-9 reps)

Leg Day

  • Squats(3 sets of 8-12 reps)
  • Lunges(2 laps, create your own track. For e.g. The length of the a free-weight section or fitness studio)
  • Leg Press(3 sets of 7-10 reps)
  • Hamstring Curls(3 sets of 6-10 reps)
  • Hip Thrusts(3 sets of 8-10 reps)

Arm Exercises

Mix these into your chest or back workouts at the end.

  • EZ-Bar Bicep Curls (3 sets of 5-9 reps)
  • Chin Ups (3-4 sets of 6-10 reps)
  • Machine Curls (3 sets of 8-10 reps)
  • Tricep Extensions (3 sets of 8-10 reps)
  • Close-Grip Curls (3 sets of 8-10 reps)
  • Tricep Dips (3 sets of 8-10 reps)
  • Tricep Pulldowns (3 sets of 8-10 reps)

Cardio + core

  • 12:3:30(12 incline, 3 speed, 30 minutes)
  • Plank (1 to 3 minutes)
  • Decline Sit-ups or Cable Crunches(10-20 reps)
  • Leg-raises ( 3 sets of 10 reps)
