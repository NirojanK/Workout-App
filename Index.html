Training Split Guide

PPL (Push Pull Legs)

Best for consistency and balanced growth. Train 5–6 days per week.

  • Push: Chest, Shoulders, Triceps
  • Pull: Back, Biceps
  • Legs: Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes

Bro Split

Focus on one muscle group per day. Great for isolating muscles.

  • Day 1: Chest
  • Day 2: Back
  • Day 3: Legs
  • Day 4: Arms

Upper / Lower Split

Great for beginners and strength focus.

  • Upper Body: Chest, Back, Arms
  • Lower Body: Legs, Glutes
